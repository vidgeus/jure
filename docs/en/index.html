Practice Areas

Specialized in all aspects of property law

Law of Obligations

Our law office provides legal assistance and advice in the law of obligations, including drafting and reviewing contracts, protecting the rights of contracting parties, debt recovery, claims for damages, and representation in court and out-of-court proceedings.

Law of Property (Rights in Rem)

Our law office provides legal assistance in all matters of the law of property, including the acquisition and protection of ownership, land registry proceedings, the settlement of property-law relationships, and representation in court and out-of-court proceedings.

Inheritance Law

Our law office provides legal assistance in all matters of inheritance, the drafting of wills, probate proceedings, and the protection of heirs' rights in court and out-of-court proceedings.

Administrative Proceedings

Our law office provides legal assistance in administrative proceedings before the competent authorities of the Republic of Croatia, with a particular focus on resolving the status of foreign nationals in Croatia (approval of temporary or permanent residence, family reunification).

An exception to the above are documents you have entrusted for safekeeping, which are kept on the basis of your instruction.

Taking into account the specific features of the legal profession, the controller may in individual cases, and depending on the particular circumstances, set a longer retention period for the file in order to protect legitimate and legal interests.

If mandatory legal regulations require a longer retention period, your data will be deleted upon the expiry of the additional statutory periods.

The personal data of data subjects and business partners is kept for five years, except in the case of establishing or defending legal claims or where a mandatory legal regulation prescribes a longer retention period.

Your rights

Right of access – you may request confirmation of whether your data is being processed, for what purpose and to what extent.

Right to rectification – where your personal data being processed is inaccurate or incomplete.

Right to erasure – you may request the erasure of your personal data if the purpose for which it was collected no longer exists, if the processing is unlawful, if the processing disproportionately interferes with your protected legitimate interests, or if the processing is based on your consent which you have withdrawn. Nevertheless, account must be taken of the possible existence of other grounds that may preclude the full erasure of your personal data, for example storage expressly prescribed by law, or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims and the like.

Right to data portability – data you have provided that is processed on the basis of your consent or for the performance of a contract, and whose processing is carried out by automated means, will, at your request, be forwarded to you in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format. Where technically feasible, it may, at your request, be transmitted directly to another controller.

Right to restriction of processing – you have the right to request the restriction of the processing of your data:

  • if you contest the accuracy of your personal data, for a period enabling us to verify the accuracy of the data;
  • if the processing is unlawful but you have refused erasure and instead request the restriction of the processing;
  • if your personal data is no longer needed for the intended purpose but you still require it for the establishment or defence of legal claims;
  • if you have objected to the processing of personal data, pending verification of whether the controller's legitimate grounds override yours.

Right to object – you may object at any time to the processing of your personal data pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation, i.e. where your personal data is processed in the public interest or the processing is based on the needs of our legitimate interests. When exercising your rights, please ensure that you provide evidence that allows your identity to be established beyond doubt (a personal identification document).

Right to lodge a complaint – if you consider that, in processing your personal data, data-protection regulations have been breached and your interests, rights and freedoms have thereby been infringed, please contact this office so that we can clarify any ambiguities.
